# JustStart goals

Date: 2026-10-22 | Status: Phase 0 public discovery authorized; MVP A prioritized for validation, not implementation

## Outcome

Help adults with ADHD begin and resume meaningful everyday actions with less friction, while preserving autonomy and dignity. Adult ADHD is the Founder-confirmed initial audience; prioritize the English market while retaining a future English/Chinese capability. Similar executive-function difficulties remain a possible future audience, not the initial research population. Success means useful action outside the app, not a larger task list or more time in chat.

## Proposed measurement framework

No baseline or measured result exists. Thresholds below are planning proposals, subject to Founder approval before research.

- Primary: self-reported action started within five minutes of an explicit attempt beginning. Report starts / all eligible attempts, with participant count and missing reports separately; a button click alone is not proof of action.
- Secondary: time to first real-world action, perceived effort (optional 1–5 rating), useful-next-step acceptance, interruption recovery, voluntary reuse and task progress.
- Guardrails: unwanted pressure, distress reports, unsafe suggestions, abandonment, notification burden, opt-outs, privacy incidents, accessibility failures and model cost per helped attempt.
- No symptom-reduction, diagnostic or treatment-success KPI. No optimization for compulsive engagement or longest streak.
- Stage-specific definitions and proposed directional thresholds: [discovery plan](docs/product/discovery-plan.md).

## Scope control

This package establishes research and delivery capability. The Founder has approved the operating model and public-source parallel research. Three independent research reports and a cross-reviewed synthesis inform a candidate MVP A; B/C remain long-term directions. See the [research synthesis](docs/research/phase-00-research-synthesis.md). This work does not validate demand, prove efficacy, choose pricing or approve a stack. No application code, deployment, accounts, real-user data collection, commits, pushes or external issue creation in this authorization.
