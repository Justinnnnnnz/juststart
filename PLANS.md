# JustStart phase roadmap

Date: 2026-10-22 | Current state: Phase 0 initial organization/workflow accepted; public-source multi-agent research authorized; participant research and Phase 1 not authorized

Phases are decision gates, not dates or implicit authorization. No feature implementation, deployment, spending, external issues, commits or pushes is approved by this roadmap.

| Phase | Goal | Deliverables | Dependencies | Founder gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Product Discovery | Understand the job, risks, alternatives and testable value | Organization/workflow, evidence and hypothesis registers, approved studies, eventual synthesis and MVP decision brief | Initial vision; approved research protocol/resources before user research | Review initial package; separately authorize research; approve evidence sufficiency before Phase 1 |
| 1 — Product Definition | Turn evidence into a bounded product | Versioned PRD, non-goals, stories/acceptance, prioritized backlog, business assumptions and metric targets | Sufficient Phase 0 evidence and explicit entry approval | Approve MVP scope, PRD, success/guardrail criteria and Phase 2 entry |
| 2 — UX & Architecture | Make behavior and delivery design reviewable | Journeys, wireframes/design system, accessible prototype findings, ADRs, OpenAPI, data/threat model, evaluation and cost plans | Approved Phase 1 scope; expert availability | Approve UX, architecture/stack/providers, data policy and implementation plan before Phase 3 |
| 3 — MVP Implementation | Build small approved vertical slices | Reviewed code, contracts/migrations, automated checks, AI evaluations and operational documentation | Approved Phase 2 packet; explicit code/Git/external-tool permissions | Accept MVP candidate and authorize beta preparation; no automatic release |
| 4 — QA & Private Beta | Check safety, reliability and useful action | Regression/accessibility/security findings, professionally reviewed safety flows, consented beta evidence, support/rollback readiness | MVP candidate; approved hosting/data handling; qualified reviews | Authorize beta exposure first; later approve launch readiness and residual risks |
| 5 — Launch & Iteration | Deliver safely and learn from real outcomes | Explicitly approved deployment, monitored metrics/costs, incident process, feedback-driven backlog and experiments | Phase 4 readiness, commercial/privacy approvals and release permission | Approve launch, pricing/claims and material changes; later iterations remain gated |

## Current execution boundary

The [Phase 0 plan](plans/phase-00-product-discovery.md) separates this completed initial setup from future research. Research findings may lead to narrowing, repeating discovery or stopping. No schedule is credible before resources, participant access and reviewer availability are agreed. The [decision register](docs/organization/decision-rights.md) is the approval source of record.

## Product horizons within the phase gates

A/B/C describe product breadth; Phases 0–5 describe delivery maturity. They are different axes. Approval to validate A does not approve building B or C, and completing a delivery phase does not automatically expand product breadth.

| Horizon | Retained direction | Condition before scope expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — priority validation | Low-friction first action and a minimal same-attempt recovery concept | Founder reviews candidate scope and evidence; manual/rules/AI comparison; no code yet |
| B — support during action | AI body doubling; optional bounded check-ins; immediate rewards and personalized gamification | Evidence of need beyond A; autonomy/burden/cost tests and explicit scope approval |
| B/C — reviewed skills and adaptation | CBT/ACT-informed skills; user-declared emotion/energy adaptation; personalized behavioral strategies | Exact-strategy evidence, qualified review, consent/privacy and independent safety evaluation |
| C — broader life support | AI daily planning; learning/work assistance; long-term life management and cross-context personalization | Repeated value across separately validated jobs, integration necessity and approved data/operating model |

No long-term direction has been removed. [Three-agent research synthesis](docs/research/phase-00-research-synthesis.md) records current recommendations and counterevidence.
