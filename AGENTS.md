## Working discipline

1. Read [goals](GOALS.md), [phase plan](PLANS.md), relevant documents and [decision rights](docs/organization/decision-rights.md).
2. Separate source-backed evidence, observations, hypotheses and proposals. Cite direct URLs and research dates. Never fabricate interviews, clinical results, market findings or approvals.
3. Work from a bounded task with owner, allowed paths, inputs, acceptance criteria and dependencies. Prefer one active coordinator and temporary specialist roles; do not maintain an agent per team.
4. Use parallel agents only for explicitly authorized, independent work with non-overlapping file ownership. A fresh reviewer must independently review future product code. AI review does not replace clinical/legal expertise.
5. Keep changes reviewable. Follow [workflow](docs/engineering/development-workflow.md) and [definition of done](docs/engineering/definition-of-done.md). No product implementation until all applicable phase gates are approved.
6. Follow [safety principles](docs/clinical/safety-principles.md): no diagnosis, treatment claims, prescription advice, coercion or hidden sensitive-data collection.
7. Log proposed decisions and unresolved risks. Approval must be an explicit Founder instruction, with date, scope and evidence recorded; never infer it from silence or a document's existence.
8. Report changes, checks, limitations, actual Subagent contributions, cross-review outcomes and pending approvals. At the end of this public-research round, stop for Founder review. No real users have been recruited or studied by this team.
