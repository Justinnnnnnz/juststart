# Product vision

Date: 2026-10-22 | Status: adult ADHD / English-market discovery direction approved; not an approved PRD

## Vision and audience

JustStart initially targets adults with ADHD, helping turn an overwhelming intention into a manageable next action, then recover when interrupted. Adults with similar executive-function difficulties may be a future audience. A diagnosis is not proposed as an eligibility requirement, and the product must not infer one.

Initial segment hypothesis: adults who know what they want to do but repeatedly stall on low-risk work, study or household tasks. Research must distinguish unclear tasks, task aversion, external constraints, fatigue and need for professional support rather than assuming every delay reflects ADHD. The Founder confirmed English-market priority while retaining future English/Chinese support; a specific launch country, platform and commercial segment remain decisions. Design localization-ready copy and layouts without adding bilingual delivery to MVP A by default. Minors are outside the proposed initial audience.

## Job to be done

“When I know what matters but feel stuck, help me choose one action small enough to begin, without adding planning work or making me feel judged.”

A second candidate job: “When I lose my place, help me recover context and choose a restart point.” These are hypotheses, not interview findings.

## Candidate journey

Recognize being stuck → enter a short intention → optionally identify a blocker → choose/edit one small action → attempt it outside the app → optionally report progress, pause or restart. Keep each step skippable where safe. Offer a manual path if AI is unavailable. Validate the journey before detailed interface design.

## Principles

- Real-world usefulness over planning volume, chat length or engagement.
- A small next action over an overwhelming generated checklist.
- User edits and choice over opaque automated schedules.
- Respectful language, low sensory load, accessible mobile flows and easy interruption recovery.
- Transparent AI limitations and no claim that AI presence is equivalent to a human companion.
- Data minimization; task text can contain highly sensitive information even without health fields.

## Boundaries and differentiation hypothesis

This is daily-life execution support, not diagnosis, psychotherapy, crisis care or medication advice. Do not promise treatment or use clinical improvement as a marketing claim.

Task breakdown, visual planning, self-care rewards and human coworking already have competitors; see [research plan](competitor-research-plan.md). The proposed opportunity is low-friction assistance at the moment of being stuck and after interruption. Whether that is valuable, distinctive and worth paying for is unknown.

## Business assumptions to test

Potential buyer: the adult user. Subscription, one-time purchase and free core with paid extras are alternatives, not chosen models. First test repeated usefulness, accessibility and willingness to pay; estimate AI cost per useful attempt before proposing prices. No advertising, sale of sensitive data or paid acquisition is authorized.

## Long-term vision retained

MVP A is the first value-validation focus, not the final product boundary. Preserve optional AI body doubling, immediate rewards and personalized gamification, professionally reviewed CBT/ACT-informed skills, user-declared emotion/energy adaptation, AI daily planning, learning/work assistance, long-term life management and personalized behavioral strategies. B/C sequencing and prerequisites are in the [roadmap](../../PLANS.md); no blanket feature approval is implied. The [research synthesis](../research/phase-00-research-synthesis.md) translates current evidence into reviewable candidate interactions.
