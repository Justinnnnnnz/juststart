# Competitor research plan and initial source inventory

Research date: 2026-10-22 (America/Toronto) | Status: preliminary public desk research

## Method and evidence rules

Separate provider-described capabilities, observed product behavior, independent user feedback and our hypotheses. Official pages can establish what a vendor offers or claims, not user outcomes or treatment efficacy. No account was created, subscription started or app hands-on tested. No interviews or systematic review mining have been performed. Prices and platform availability can change; re-check before a business decision.

## Research work packages (future)

1. Product/UX: map capture → plan → start → focus → interruption → resume → feedback using public documentation first. Only test authenticated flows after approval; record platform/version and what was actually observed.
2. Growth: compare monetization, onboarding, cancellation and positioning; record country, currency, taxes, billing interval and capture date. Do not infer market size or willingness to pay from download/rating claims.
3. UX: proposed balanced sample of 20 recent public reviews per core product across positive/mixed/negative experiences where available, plus older negative cases; document selection, dates, versions and duplicates. This is qualitative, non-representative sampling. Avoid retaining usernames, health disclosures or copied sensitive narratives; collect sanitized themes and source references under an approved protocol.
4. Safety/Privacy: inspect public policies, AI disclosures, deletion/export options and safety claims. Document unknowns without declaring a competitor insecure or compliant.
5. Product: triangulate review themes with future interviews and prototypes; log counterevidence and substitution risk. Prioritize a job, not a feature checklist.

## Comparison schema and outputs

For each claim capture product, user/job, exact capability, source URL, access date, fact vs vendor claim vs inference, platform, pricing context, feedback method, limitations, confidence, owner and recheck date. Deliver a journey matrix, feedback theme ledger, pricing snapshot and ranked opportunity hypotheses. Refresh volatile facts before each scope or pricing decision.

The first differentiation test is whether one-action assistance produces faster useful action with less burden than existing tools. If not, narrow or stop the idea rather than add more features.

## Deeper research handoff

The approved PM Subagent round is recorded in [adult ADHD needs and competition](../research/adult-adhd-needs-and-competition.md), with dated direct sources and capability-level limits. The initial inventory above is a baseline, not the final competitive analysis.

A material correction to any “missing one-step execution” narrative: [Goblin Tools Taskmaster](https://goblin.tools/Taskmaster), checked 2026-10-22, explicitly describes one-at-a-time execution, estimated timers, next-task suggestions and breaking down the current task when stuck. Therefore neither AI decomposition nor a one-step screen establishes unique differentiation. Direct public documentation was read; no authenticated hands-on comparison or representative complaint study has been performed. Friction, quality and reliability gaps are research questions, not proven competitor defects.
